## 1. Getting Started

### Sign Up & Log In
- Create an account with email, password, username, and full name.
- Log in with email and password.
- Forgot your password? Use the password reset flow to get a recovery email.

### What Happens on First Login
- Your profile is automatically created.
- You're taken to the home feed.
- Your activity status starts updating so friends can see when you're online.

---

## 2. Home Feed

The home feed is the first thing you see after logging in.

- **Posts from people you follow** appear in a scrollable feed.
- **Stories bar** at the top shows stories from people you follow (tap to view).
- **Daily challenge card** appears showing today's creative prompt.
- **Pull down to refresh** the feed.
- **Tap the top** to scroll back to the beginning.
- Each post shows: the image, caption, creator info, like/save/react buttons, comment count.
- **Like** a post with a single tap on the heart.
- **Save** a post to your collections.
- **React** with emojis: like, love, haha, wow, sad, fire, or clap.
- Posts you scroll past are automatically tracked for view insights.

## 3. AI Image Generation

The core feature of Artigen — create art using AI models.

### How It Works
1. Go to the **Generate** screen (camera tab → generate).
2. Type a text prompt describing what you want.
3. Pick an AI model.
4. Choose an aspect ratio.
5. Tap **Generate**.
6. The image is created and shown to you. You can then post it, remix it, or try again.

### Available AI Models

## 4. Advanced Creative Tools

Beyond basic text-to-image generation, Artigen offers a suite of creative tools.

### Remix
- Take any post's prompt and modify it to create your own version.
- The original prompt is shown so you can tweak it.
- Remix attribution links back to the original post.
- Browse all remixes of a post in a grid view.

### Restyle
- Apply a style preset to an existing image.
- See a before/after preview.
- Choose from a grid of style presets.

### Animate
- Turn a static image into an animation.
- Pick from different animation types.
- The result is saved as a video.

### Inpaint
- Draw a mask over part of an image.
- Describe what should fill the masked area.
- Great for fixing or modifying specific parts of an image.

### Outpaint
- Extend an image beyond its original borders.
- Choose the direction to expand (up, down, left, right).
- Set how many pixels to add.

### Upscale
- Make an image larger and sharper.
- Uses Real-ESRGAN AI upscaling.
- Choose the scale factor.

### ControlNet
- Use a control image (pose, depth map, or edges) to guide generation.
- Adjust control strength.
- Supports multiple control types and preset grids.

### Comic Generator
- Create comic-style sequential art panels.

## 5. Stories

Short-lived content that disappears after 24 hours.

- **View stories** by tapping on profile circles at the top of the feed.
- Stories play automatically and progress to the next one.
- **Reply to stories** via chat.
- **Create stories** from the camera tab.
- **Interactive stickers** you can add to stories:
  - Poll sticker (vote on options)
  - Question sticker (ask me anything)
  - Quiz sticker (multiple choice)
  - Emoji slider sticker (rate with emoji)
  - Countdown sticker (event countdown)
  - Link sticker (swipe-up links)
- **Close friends ring** shows stories shared only with your close friends list.

## 6. Posting & Sharing

### Creating a Post
1. Take a photo with the camera or pick from your gallery.
2. Or generate an AI image first.
3. Add a caption.
4. Optionally add:
   - **Location tag** — tag where the art was made.
   - **Challenge submission** — enter a daily challenge.
   - **Schedule** — publish at a later time.
   - **AI metadata** — model used, style, and prompt are saved automatically for AI-generated images.

### Reels
- Short vertical videos in a scrollable feed.
- Like and save reels just like regular posts.

### Scheduled Posts
- Set a date/time for your post to go live.
- View and manage all scheduled posts from a dedicated screen.

### Cross-Posting
- Share your post to external platforms.
- Configure which platforms to cross-post to in settings.
- Track the status of each cross-post (posting, posted, or failed).

## 7. Explore & Search

- Discover new content from the community.
- Filter to show only AI-generated posts.
- Masonry grid layout for browsing.

### Search
- **Search posts** by keyword.
- **Search by prompt** — find images made from similar prompts.
- **Search by model** — find images made with a specific AI model.
- **Search users** by username or display name.
- **Search hashtags** — browse posts tagged with a specific hashtag.

### Visual Search

### Explore Map
- Browse posts on a map by location.
- Discover art from around the world.

## 9. Comments & Reactions

### Comments
- Comment on any post.
- **Nested replies** — reply to a specific comment to create a thread.
- **Like comments** — tap to like, batch-check which comments you've liked.

### Reactions

## 12. Challenges & Battles

### Daily Challenges
- A new creative prompt is generated every day.
- The prompt includes a theme, description, and style suggestion.
- Submit your entry by creating a post tagged to the challenge.
- View all entries in a grid.
- Vote on your favorites.
- Leaderboard shows top creators.

### Art Battles
- **1v1 art battles** — two artists compete with the same prompt.
- Each artist submits their creation.
- Community votes to decide the winner.
- **Battle timer** counts down the voting period.
- The battle creator can finalize results after voting ends.
- **Tie detection** if votes are equal.
- Create your own battles or join existing ones.

### Weekly Events
- Special weekly creative events with unique themes.
- Event countdown timer.
- RSVP to events.
- View attendees.

## 22. Provenance & Authenticity

Artigen supports content authenticity and provenance tracking.

- **C2PA compliance** — artwork gets a digital signature proving when and how it was created.
- **Content hash** — unique fingerprint of the artwork.
- **Prompt hash** — hash of the prompt used.
- **HMAC-SHA-256 signature** — cryptographic proof of authenticity.
- **Provenance badge** appears on posts with verified provenance.
- **Provenance sheet** — tap the badge to see full provenance details including the manifest and verification status.
- **AI disclosure** — transparent labeling of AI-generated content.
